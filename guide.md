# Flamenco Guitar Guide

Flamenco is more than music — it is a living story of rhythm, memory, and feeling.

## Important ideas

- It often begins with the singer, dancer, and guitarist responding to each other.
- The guitar provides the pulse, melody, and dramatic tension.
- Every performance has a sense of improvisation and connection.

## A short practice checklist

1. Listen to the compás before playing.
2. Keep your wrist relaxed.
3. Let the silence carry emotion.
4. Match the energy of the dancer or singer.

## Quick note

The best flamenco playing feels like a conversation, not a performance checklist.
