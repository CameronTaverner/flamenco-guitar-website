Origins

Flamenco emerged from the cultural crossroads of Gypsy, Moorish, Jewish, and Spanish traditions. It grew in the streets, cafes, and courtyards of Andalusia.

Core Elements

  • Compás — the rhythmic pulse
  • Palmas — hand clapping
  • Baile — dance movement
  • Cante — singing

Why it matters

The guitar is not just accompaniment. It is a voice of emotion, improvisation, and dramatic timing.

Flamenco Forms
