Essential Flamenco Techniques

A skilled flamenco guitarist combines precision, expression, and a strong internal pulse.

1. Rasgueado

A quick strumming pattern that creates the characteristic fiery texture of flamenco.

  • Fast down-up movements
  • Used for excitement and tension
  • Often heard in dance sections

2. Picado

Fast scale-like runs that make the guitar sound light, agile, and lyrical.

  1. Use relaxed wrist motion
  2. Keep the tone even
  3. Match the phrasing of the singer

Key Practice Notes

  • Listen to the compás before playing
  • Let silence be part of the phrase
  • Respect the dancer and singer
  • Use dynamics to create drama
